# Directive

A directive is a legally binding goal that all [[EU Member States]] must achieve, but the method to achieving it is left to said member state.

This is a different approach compared to a [[Regulation]]

## References

1. [Definition of a directive](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/EN/legal-content/glossary/directive.html)

> "A directive is a legal act adopted by the EU institutions addressed to the EU Member States and, as laid down in Article [288](http://data.europa.eu/eli/treaty/tfeu_2016/art_288/oj/eng) of the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union, is binding as to the result to be achieved."

> "The national authorities of each EU country to which the directive is addressed determine the form and the methods they use to incorporate the directive into their national law (formally known as ‘transposition’). Generally, this needs to be done within 2 years of the directive’s adoption."

# FT-IR Spectroscopy

Fourier Transform Infrared Spectroscopy (FT-IR Spectroscopy) measures how textiles absorb infrared radiation. When a textile is exposed to infrared radiation, it absorbs wavelengths across the infrared range based on its molecular structure. This process results in a spectrum unique to the textile; similar to a fingerprint. By comparing this fingerprint with known reference samples, it is possible to identify textile fibre types.

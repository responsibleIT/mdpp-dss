# Circular Economy Action Plan

Consists of both legislative and non-legislative plans addressing the life-cycle of products, such as batteries, textiles, and more. Part of this action plan is the legally binding [[Ecodesign for Sustainable Products Regulation]].

## References

1. [EU - Circular Economy](https://environment.ec.europa.eu/strategy/circular-economy_en)

> "Due for adoption in 2026 the Circular Economy Act aims to establish a Single Market for secondary raw materials, increase the supply of high-quality recycled materials and stimulate demand for these materials within the EU."

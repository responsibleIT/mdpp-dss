# European Green deal

This deal was created with the intent of the EU becoming the world's first climate neutral continent while supporting the economy. By 2030, the goal is to achieve at least a 55% reduction in [[Greenhouse gas emissions (GHG)]] compared to 1990 levels; a goal set based on "a comprehensive impact assessment".

This deal contains plans for: the economy, energy, industry, and transport. This deal is legally binding for [[EU Member States]] through the adoption of the [[EU Climate Law]].

There are several initiatives that have (and still are) taken place. One of which is the [[Circular Economy Action Plan]], which consists of both legislative and non-legislative plans addressing the life-cycle of products, such as batteries, textiles, and more.

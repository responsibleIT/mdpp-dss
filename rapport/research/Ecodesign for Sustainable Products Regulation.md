# Ecodesign for Sustainable Products Regulation

This is one of the initiatives in line with the [[European Green deal]] goal of achieving net 0 [[Greenhouse gas emissions (GHG)]] by 2050 within the EU. This regulation is a legislative tool, part of the [[Circular Economy Action Plan]], which allows for the plans to be addressed.

This replaces the old [[Directive]], which only covered energy-related products (such as washing machines and insulation). This new regulation covers almost all physical products placed on the EU market, with some exceptions like food, feed, and medicine.

This new regulation includes mandating products be repairable, as well as improving the durability of products. Other things like water efficiency are targeted as well.

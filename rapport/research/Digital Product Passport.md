# Digital Product Passport

This is a digital document which contains information on several things about a product:

1. Where the product was made
2. How the product was made
3. What materials were used
4. Its environmental impact
5. How the product can be recycled or disposed of

This is based on a new EU regulation -- the [[Ecodesign for Sustainable Products Regulation]] -- which was proposed by the [[European Commission]] on the 30th of March 2022 as part of the Sustainable Products Initiative.
